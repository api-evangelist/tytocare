# TytoCare

> TytoCare is an AI-powered remote physical examination and virtual care company. Its FDA-cleared handheld examination device and Home Smart Clinic / Pro Smart Clinic platforms let clinicians examine heart, lungs, ears, throat, skin, and temperature remotely, serving 240+ healthcare organizations and 1.6M+ patients. TytoCare publishes no public developer portal, API documentation, or client SDKs; its integrations (EHR/telehealth) are delivered through private partner channels.
